How the radar scores

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth × momentum

Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −80…+200%, weighted 0.6. Maturity rises with the days DefiLlama has tracked the protocol, from 0.70 to 1.20 over four years. Breadth adds 2% per extra chain, up to five. Momentum adds 8% when growth is +25% or better and takes 8% away when it is −25% or worse. Every number comes from the sealed snapshot alone — no name is ever special-cased, and the page recomputes each row's value from the same figures the board carries.

“Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no token for the protocol; it is not proof that none exists. Protocols whose token already trades sit in the Already trading view at the market's own value, never in the ranking. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of a token that trades are left out of the board. Implied value is this entry's own claim, never one make.fun makes, and it does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
